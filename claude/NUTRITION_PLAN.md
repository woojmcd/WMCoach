## Rules
1. Surplus capped at +100–200 kcal; gain 0.1–0.2 %BW/wk (0.2–0.35 lb/wk). −100 kcal if >0.35 %/wk for 2 check-ins or navel waist up >0.5 in in 4 weeks.
2. Protein ~1.0–1.1 g/lb in 5 feedings of 23–53 g, 3–5 h apart.
3. ~75% of carbs between lunch and post-workout; pre-workout low in fat and fiber.
4. No meal >~650 kcal; fiber spread across the day; 3.5–4 L fluid mostly between meals; steady daily sodium.

Granola modelled at 40 g = ~4P/27C/7F (~185 kcal); instant oatmeal at ~4P/32C/2.5F per flavored packet. Check labels.
Dave's Killer Bread 21 Whole Grains: 1 slice (45 g) = 110 kcal, 6P/22C/1.5F. Thin-sliced version (28 g) = 60 kcal, 3P/14C/1F; use 3 thin slices instead of 2 regular.

### If the gym time moves
- Lift at lunch: M1 → pre-workout ~10:30 → post-workout ~1:30 → turkey/beef + rice as dinner ~6:00 → evening snack.
- Lift 7–8 pm: pre-workout ~5:30–6:30, post-workout by ~9:00, then fold the yogurt into the post meal or skip it.
- The anchors are fixed: pre-workout 60–120 min before lifting, post-workout within ~2 h after. Daily protein total matters more than a 30-min window.

## Sat–Sun (best effort)

- Big dinner planned → skip the pre-workout meal (−~385–475 kcal of room).
