**Cardio:** 40 min of stair climbing on most days (5 sessions that week), plus a swim (1,000 m / 35 min).

**Program traits**
- It is a 5-day body-part split (pull / push / calves+core / legs / arms+core).
- Upper and leg days open with a 4-stretch mobility circuit.
- Rest is **90 s** between sets throughout, with **2–3 working sets** per exercise.
- Cable isolation work uses ascending-weight rep pyramids (15/12/10, 20/15/10, 15/12/8).
- Main compounds use a **top set + backoff sets**.
- Core is trained twice a week and calves once.

**How it differs from A/B**
- Push and pull are each trained twice a week, legs once, and arms are folded into the push/pull days.
- The warm-up stretch circuits are gone.
- **Same-weight supersets to failure** are the signature move: rope extensions → rope pushdowns, reverse cable curls → cable bar curls, and seated → standing lateral raises.
- There's 15 s rest between superset partners and 90 s elsewhere.
- More **top set + backoff** work, with the backoff about 30% lighter.
- Technique cues are written into the prescriptions.
- Calves and shrugs are dropped.
