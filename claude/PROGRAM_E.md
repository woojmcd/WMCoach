# Program E: Functional Bodybuilding hybrid ("unit" build)

**Status:** in the app since 0.9.0 (approved by Walter 2026-11-14). Built from `data/programs/TRAIN-E26.json` into `data/program.json`; Program E's week 1 is Mon 16 Nov 2026 (a new program starts a new block). Program C ran before it and is kept in `data/training_history.json`.
**Skeleton:** Mon Push / Tue Pull / Wed Legs / Thu Push / Fri Pull, the same structure as Program C. Keeps the same-weight supersets, top + backoff sets and 90 s rest.

## Goals
1. **Size:** get to 180+ lb and *hold* it as a natural lifter. Walter has about 4 consistent training years, so there's real room left. DEXA baseline before the bulk, rescan at 12–16 weeks.
2. **Lower-body priority:** vastus lateralis (outer quad sweep) and hip abductors (glute med; hip width).
3. **Upper-body priority:** upper traps and upper-back thickness (the "football" look), plus side delts.
4. **Abs:** train for muscle growth with loaded, progressive work, 3×/week.
5. **Posture:** scapular pulling outweighs pressing about 2:1, plus postural endurance work (carries, long squeezes).
6. **Constraints:** legs 1×/week to protect running; no calves (foot tendinosis, already large); Mon–Fri, ≤60 min, ≥90 s rest unless noted. Running is the conditioning, so no metcon finishers that would cut into leg recovery.

## The 4 non-negotiable mechanical rules (revised for the actual hernia threshold)
1. **Exhale through the hard part: no Valsalva, no belt.** The set ends the moment the waistband or groin pinch shows up. Don't push through it.
2. **Load ceiling on spinal loading.** Symptoms start around 315 on squats and deadlifts, so barbell squats are capped at **~225 lb** total load at the top, **6+ reps, 3 reps in reserve (RIR 3)**, and are secondary work only. No maxes, no grinding, ever. Every other heavy lift is chest-supported, back-padded or hand-supported.
3. **Ribs down, pelvis neutral.** No rep finishes in a low-back arch. Ab flexion means curling the spine toward the pelvis while exhaling. Hanging work means curling the pelvis up, never swinging.
4. **Pull beats push.** Scapular pulling sets are about 2× pressing sets. Every upper day opens with a rotator or lower-trap primer. No loaded end-range stretch on the pecs or lats.

**Stop rule:** if a bulge won't go back in, or the pain is sharp or getting worse, stop training and call your surgeon the same day.

## Banned mechanics
| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| Barbell squats above ~225 lb, under 6 reps, or ground out; any max testing | Above the threshold where intra-abdominal pressure (IAP) triggers symptoms |
| Barbell deadlifts (all variants), barbell RDLs, good mornings | Aggravates the low back, and adds nothing you need: hamstrings come from curls, back from chest-supported rows |
| Lifting belts, Valsalva | Built to maximize IAP, which pushes on the inguinal canal |
| Barbell hip thrusts or any load across the hip crease | Direct pressure over the canal |
| Hyperextensions to failure | Loaded low-back extension feeds anterior pelvic tilt |
| Kipping or swinging leg raises, GHD or decline weighted sit-ups | Momentum-driven hip-flexor jerk at the groin |
| Barbell bent-over rows, Pendlay rows, unsupported T-bar rows | Low back held under load |
| Standing overhead press, full overhead triceps extensions | Tight lats force a low-back arch overhead |
| Chest dips, deep-stretch flyes, cable lat pullovers, upright rows, anything behind the neck | Loads the rounded-shoulder pattern at end range |
| Calf raises (excluded, not banned on mechanics) | Foot tendinosis; calves already big |

Tempo notation: lower–pause–lift–squeeze, in seconds. Superset = 15 s between partners, 90 s after each round.

### Mon: Push A, side delts + upper-chest press (~60 min)
| # | Lift (alt) | Sets × reps | Rest / tempo | Targets | Mechanical justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rope Face Pull, finish rotated out (band pull-apart) | 2 × 15, RIR 3 | 60 s · 2-0-1-2 | Rotator cuff, rear delt, mid trap | Primer, so shorter rest on purpose. Pulls the shoulders back before pressing |
| 2 | Single-Arm Lean-Away Cable Lateral (machine lateral) | 4 × 12–15/side | 90 s · 2-0-1-1 | Side delt | Priority first while fresh. The cable loads the bottom of the range, and raising ~30° forward avoids impingement |
| 3 | Low-Incline DB Press, 15–30° (plate-loaded incline machine) | 2 × 8–10 + 1 × 12 @ ~70% | 90–120 s · 3-1-1-0 · RIR 1–2 | Upper chest | Main lift. | 4 | Machine Fly / Pec Deck, stop at the shoulder line (single-arm cable fly) | 3 × 12–15 | 90 s · 3-0-1-1 | Chest | Chest volume without a loaded end-range stretch on tight pecs. Machine over a two-stack cable fly because paired cable stations are hard to get |

**Ab progression:** cable crunch uses double progression, 8–12 reps; add load when all sets hit 12. Hanging knee raise: once 3 × 15 is clean, hold a DB between the feet. Woodchop: add load at 12/side. Tuesday cable crunch is the lighter day (12–15 reps; add load at 15) and Wednesday's is the heavy day (8–12), so the same exercise isn't hit at the same intensity twice. Abs are trained 4×/week because they're a stated priority and the cost is low: Walter's hernia is symptom-free below his heaviest squat/deadlift loads, so ab work isn't limited by it. Program C was a cut program, so its ab results don't predict growth in a surplus. Review at the 12–16 week DEXA: if crunch loads haven't climbed, change the exercises or rep ranges rather than adding more sessions.

**Equipment preferences:** no Smith machine (DB or machine subs throughout); single cable stations are easy to get, but two side-by-side stacks are not, so no exercise needs a paired cable setup (machine flies; single-arm cable variants as alternates).

**Main lifts for app progression:** Mon low-incline DB press, Tue chest-supported row, Wed leg press, Thu machine chest press, Fri single-arm DB row. The chain squat progresses in reps and tempo only once it reaches the 225 lb ceiling.

## Daily posture micro-dose (not a lift day)
Two to three times a day: 15 band pull-aparts and 10 wall slides. Cue "ribs down, chest tall, shoulder blades in back pockets" at every doorway. If the trap soreness from standing isn't easing after 6–8 weeks, get a physical therapist to assess it.

## How the app runs it (0.9.0)
- Lifts kept from Program C on the same day keep their names (e.g. "Reverse Cable Curls", "Seated Hamstring Curls", "Single Arm DB Rows"), so their history and targets carry over; the cue says how Program E does them. New lifts start with a calibration set.
- The chain squat never gets a target above 225 lb; once there, the coach asks for a slower lowering and a pause instead of load.
- The carry is logged in metres (30–40 m each side).
- The Log tab shows each day's focus and a "Program E rules" sheet (the four rules and the stop rule).
- The travel workout swaps every machine, cable and barbell lift for a dumbbell or bodyweight one, and keeps the banned list above.
